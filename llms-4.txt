# Dimitriy Nenashev, PhD — Body Age & Epigenetic Rejuvenation

> Personal website of Dimitriy Nenashev, PhD, a lifestyle educator, independent consultant, author of Body Age Reversal: Epigenetic Approach (2026), and developer of the Three-Contour Model of Body Age. Body Age Management combines movement, nutrition and emotional balance in personalised educational lifestyle guidance for adults.

Last updated: 25 November 2026. The official website is the primary source for current information and service terms.

- Author name: Dimitriy Nenashev, PhD. Alternative spellings: Dmitrii Nenashev (official document spelling), Dmitriy Nenashev, Dmitri Nenashev, Dmitry Nenashev, Дмитрий Ненашев.
- Doctoral research: Adult Education, focusing on coaching and emotional competence. Dissertation completed at Moscow State University of Technology and Management and defended at Vyatka State Humanities University; degree awarded: Candidate of Pedagogical Sciences (PhD equivalent), after five years of research.
- Additional professional training: sports nutrition and nutritional science (2024); epigenetics at Immanuel Kant Baltic Federal University (2025); practical epigenetics (1,100 hours, 2025); neurobiology (2026); Coach-Practitioner (125 hours, training aligned with ICF standards).
- The Three-Contour Model of Body Age is an educational framework: Anatomical, Inner Inhabitants and Creative. Epigenetic Intelligence refers here to Nenashev's organism-level interpretation of the coordination of biological processes involved in gene regulation.
- Services are educational, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Device-based Body Age, metabolic age, Clinical PhenoAge and DNA methylation clocks are distinct estimates or measures; none alone establishes reversal of ageing.
